Chana Masala

Yield: serves 4

2 Tbs. (30 mL) neutral oil heat until seeds sizzle fry until golden, 8-10 min sauté 1 min stir 1 min cook until oil separates, 8-10 min simmer 15-20 min; mash a few chickpeas stir in; top with cilantro
1 tsp. cumin seeds
1 large onion, finely chopped
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 Tbs. (6 g) fresh ginger, grated
1-2 green chilies, slit
1 tsp. ground coriander
1 tsp. ground cumin
1/2 tsp. ground turmeric
1 tsp. Kashmiri chili powder
14 oz. (400 g) can crushed tomatoes
1 tsp. salt
2 cans (15 oz. / 425 g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1 cup (240 mL) water
1 tsp. garam masala
1 tsp. amchur (dried mango powder)
1/4 cup (5 g) cilantro, chopped
  • If the spices start to stick or scorch, add a splash of water.
  • Mashing a few chickpeas against the side of the pot thickens the sauce.
  • Lemon juice can stand in for the amchur; add it at the end, off the heat.
  • Serve with basmati rice or flatbread. Keeps 4 days refrigerated and tastes better the next day.
