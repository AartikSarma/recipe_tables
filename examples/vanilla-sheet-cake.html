Vanilla Sheet Cake with Chocolate Frosting

Yield: one 9x13-inch cake, about 16 squares

Cake

1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened cream on medium-high 3 min beat 1 min mix on low, adding dry and milk alternately, dry first and last spread in greased 9x13-inch pan; bake 350°F (175°C) 30-35 min cool completely in pan, 1 hr
1-1/2 cups (300 g) sugar
3 large eggs
2 tsp. vanilla extract
2-1/2 cups (315 g) all-purpose flour whisk
2-1/2 tsp. baking powder
1/2 tsp. salt
1-1/4 cups (300 mL) milk

Frosting

1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened beat on low until combined beat until fluffy, 2 min
2-1/2 cups (300 g) powdered sugar
1/2 cup (40 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
3-4 Tbs. (45-60 mL) milk
1 tsp. vanilla extract
pinch salt

Assembly

cooled cake (above) spread over top
frosting (above)
  • Make the frosting while the cake cools. Frosting a warm cake melts it.
  • Have the butter, eggs, and milk at room temperature so the batter stays smooth.
  • The cake is done when a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  • Covered, the frosted cake keeps 3 days at room temperature.
