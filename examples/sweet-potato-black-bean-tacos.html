Sweet Potato and Black Bean Tacos

Yield: 12 tacos, serves 4

1 red onion, thinly sliced pour over; stand 20 min fill tortillas; top
1/2 cup (120 mL) cider vinegar heat until dissolved
1/2 cup (120 mL) water
1 Tbs. (12 g) sugar
1 tsp. salt
2 medium (600 g) sweet potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch cubes toss; roast 425°F (220°C) 25 min, turning once fold together
2 Tbs. (30 mL) olive oil
1 tsp. chili powder
1 tsp. ground cumin
1/2 tsp. salt
2 cans (15 oz. / 425 g each) black beans, drained and rinsed simmer 5 min; mash lightly
2 cloves garlic, minced
1/4 cup (60 mL) water
1/4 tsp. salt
2 ripe avocados mash
2 Tbs. (30 mL) lime juice
2 Tbs. cilantro, chopped (for guacamole)
1/2 tsp. salt
12 small (6-inch) corn tortillas warm in dry skillet, 30 s per side
1/2 cup (60 g) crumbled feta or cotija
2 Tbs. cilantro, chopped (for topping)
1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Start the onions first; they need 20 minutes to pickle. Roast the sweet potatoes while you simmer the beans and make the guacamole. Warm the tortillas last.
  • Leftover pickled onions keep 2 weeks refrigerated.
  • For a vegan version, skip the cheese.
